About us

At Cross Field Inc., we specialize in crafting engaging, visually appealing, and mentally stimulating games for players of all ages.
With 3D Match Triple Match Puzzle, we bring a fresh perspective to the classic match-3 genre, incorporating dynamic 3D object interaction, unique puzzles, and interactive gameplay mechanics.

Why We Created 3D Match Triple Match Puzzle:

  • To offer a unique, immersive take on traditional tile-matching games.

  • To enhance brain-training elements through observation and strategic thinking.

  • To create visually stunning, intuitive, and highly engaging puzzles for every player.

Join the millions of players worldwide and experience a new way to match, collect, and enjoy puzzles!
